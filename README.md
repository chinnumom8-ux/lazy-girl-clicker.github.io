## ✨ Features

- 💜 **Lofi RPG Dreamy Aesthetics**: Beautiful light purple lavender, soft baby blue, and twilight indigo color palette.
- 👧 **Custom Hand-Crafted SVG Character**: Cozy animated sleeping lazy girl with full layered hair, twin buns, soft bangs, star hairclip, and floating `Zzz` particles.
- ⚡ **Tap-to-Earn Core Loop**: Tap the character to collect energy with satisfying bounce animations, particle pops, and audio sound effects.
- 📻 **Interactive Vinyl Record Player**: An animated spinning record disc widget that unlocks and plays music in real-time.
- 🎵 **Curated Tracklist**:
  1. **Maula Mere Maula** — *Mithoon & Roopkumar Rathod*
  2. **Ishqa Ve** — *Zeeshan Ali*
  3. **Khairiyat (Slowed & Reverb)** — *Arijit Singh*
  4. **Raga of Revenge** — *Anirudh Ravichandran*
  5. **Dracula (8D Audio)** — *Tame Impala*
- 🍫 **Unlockable Rewards & Dialogue**: Spend energy on snacks and music tracks to unlock cozy dialogue lines.
- 💾 **Instant Local Persistence**: Your energy, unlocked tracks, and playback progress automatically persist in `localStorage`.
- 📱 **100% Responsive & Zero Dependencies**: Built with pure Vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with no external build tools or frameworks required.

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. **Tap the Lazy Girl** to earn Energy points.
2. **Unlock Snacks & Songs** from the store once you reach their energy costs:
   - 🍫 **Snacks**: 15 Energy
   - 🎵 **Music 1 (Maula Mere Maula)**: 40 Energy
   - 🎶 **Music 2 (Ishqa Ve)**: 90 Energy
   - 🎧 **Music 3 (Khairiyat)**: 130 Energy
   - 🎸 **Music 4 (Raga of Revenge)**: 160 Energy
   - 🦇 **Music 5 (Dracula)**: 210 Energy
3. **Spin the Record**: Unlocking any music track reveals the vinyl disc player. Click the disc or `⏸ / ▶` to toggle playback, or click `next ▸` to cycle through your unlocked tracks.

## 🚀 Live Demo & Deployment

This project is entirely self-contained inside a single file: `index.html`.

### Run Locally
Simply open `index.html` in your browser or run a simple local server:
```bash
python -m http.server 8080
```
Then visit `http://localhost:8080`.

### Deploy Online in Seconds
- **GitHub Pages**: Go to your repository **Settings** → **Pages** → Select `main` branch → **Save**.
- **Netlify / Vercel**: Drag and drop the repository folder into [Netlify Drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) or [Vercel](https://vercel.com) for instant free hosting.
